Our story

We kept running out of ideas.
So we fixed it.

DoNext is the family events service we wished existed.

The problem we kept hitting

Finding good stuff shouldn't be this hard

Every weekend, same story. You know there's good stuff happening — craft sessions at the library, forest schools, pop-up workshops — but finding it means trawling through Facebook groups, council websites, and local blogs that haven't been updated since half-term.

By the time you've pieced it together, half the morning's gone and you've defaulted to soft play again.

"Imagine if a weekly email just landed in your inbox — tailored to your family, your kids' ages, your budget, and the weather outside your window. Not a generic what's-on list, but a curated edit of the stuff that's actually right for you."

That's what DoNext does. We pull from dozens of local sources every single day — event listings, community pages, venue calendars, social feeds — and filter it down to the stuff that's actually worth your time. Not just the obvious attractions, but the niche, easy-to-miss events that make a weekend feel like you've properly nailed it.

Every recommendation is checked for accuracy: real dates, real times, real booking links. If something's sold out or cancelled, it doesn't make the cut. Events you can filter by age, cost, and type. Weather-matched every single morning so your plans always fit the day.

What makes us different

Not another listings site

Most what's-on sites are static lists that nobody maintains. DoNext is live — checked and updated every single day, built from dozens of sources that most parents wouldn't have time to find.

We don't just list what's on. We editorially pick the best, match them to the weather, and deliver them with everything you need to actually go — plain language, real costs, honest age recommendations.

Curated, not scraped

Every event is editorially reviewed — not auto-generated from a feed. We're looking for the unexpected: the community forest school, the free museum morning, the pop-up that won't appear on any tourist website.

Weather-smart every single day

Each dated report pairs its listings with a forecast snapshot. Rainy morning? Start indoors. Sunny day? Plan for time outside.

Babies to tweens — 0 to 12

Every event is tagged with the age range that will genuinely enjoy it. We never recommend a toddler group to an 8-year-old, or a forest school to a teenager.

Who this is for

Every kind of family

Families with kids aged 0–12, in all their shapes and sizes. Whether you're a two-parent household, a solo dad on a Saturday, step-families figuring out the routine, or grandparents with the kids for the day — if you need ideas that go beyond "just Google it", we're here.

Built in Cardiff, starting with Cardiff. One parent got fed up with the search — now we're building it into something every family in the city can use, with plans to bring it to more UK cities soon.
